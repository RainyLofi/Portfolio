01. about.lua

      

        

          
Hey, I'm RainyLofi. I'm a software engineer and I work full-time at Blueprint as a Partner. Most nights that means Roblox Studio, rain on the window and a lo-fi playlist on loop.

          
Most of my work lives in GAR and its Star Wars roleplay experiences: gameplay systems, quest & job loops, player economies, game UI, and the backend services and Discord tooling that keep a big community running.

          
I like owning a feature end to end: the server-side Luau, the UI players interact with, and the Node API and database behind it.

          
I do use AI tools (they're in the workflow list), but as tools, not as a substitute for knowing what I'm doing. Everything I ship gets reviewed and tested by me, and I can explain and debug every line of it.

function Rainy:ship(change)
    local draft = tools.assist(change) -- AI can draft, it can't sign off
    review(draft)                      -- every line, by me
    assert(understood(draft), "don't ship what you can't explain")
    playtest(draft)
    return commit(draft)
end

return Rainy
