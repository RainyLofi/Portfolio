Hey, I'm RainyLofi — a software engineer who spends most nights in Roblox Studio with the rain on and a lo-fi playlist looping in the background.

          
Most of my work lives in GAR and its Star Wars roleplay experiences: gameplay systems, quest & job loops, player economies, game UI, and the backend services and Discord tooling that keep a big community running.

          
I like building things end-to-end — from the Luau on the server, to the UI a player taps on, to the Node API and database behind it.
